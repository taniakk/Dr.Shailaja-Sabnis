About

A physician's eye,
a specialist's depth.

Dr. Shailaja Sabnis began her career with an M.B.B.S. from Seth G.S. Medical College & K.E.M. Hospital, followed by an M.D. in Medicine and a three-year residency spanning I.C.U., Cardiology, Endocrinology, Nephrology, and Rheumatology. In 2022, after two decades in practice, she completed a Diploma in Rheumatology from South Wales University, U.K. — sharpening a specialism she had already been practising in Mumbai since 1999.

That dual grounding — broad internal medicine paired with focused rheumatology — shapes her practice: every joint or skin complaint is evaluated against the complete physiological picture. She currently consults at Lilavati Hospital, Nanavati Max Super Speciality Hospital, Bhartiya Arogya Nidhi Hospital, and Holy Spirit Hospital, alongside her long-running private practice in Andheri East.
