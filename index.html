About

A physician's eye,
a specialist's depth.

Dr. Shailaja Sabnis began her career with an M.B.B.S. from Seth G.S. Medical College & K.E.M. Hospital, followed by an M.D. in Medicine and a three-year residency spanning I.C.U., Cardiology, Endocrinology, Nephrology and Rheumatology. In 2022, after two decades in practice, she returned to formal study to complete a Diploma in Rheumatology from South Wales University, U.K. — sharpening a specialism she had already been practising in Mumbai since 1999.

That dual grounding, broad internal medicine and focused rheumatology, shapes how she works: every joint or skin complaint is read against the whole patient, not in isolation. She currently consults at Lilavati Hospital, Nanavati Max Super Speciality Hospital, Bhartiya Arogya Nidhi Hospital and Holy Spirit Hospital, alongside her long-running private practice in Andheri East.
