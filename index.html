About The Physician

Comprehensive expertise in
autoimmune & joint disorders.

Dr. Shailaja Sabnis is a senior Consultant Physician and Rheumatologist with over two decades of clinical experience in Mumbai. She completed her M.D. in General Medicine in Mumbai following an extensive 3-year residency at L.T.M.G. Hospital (Sion), gaining multi-disciplinary expertise across ICU care, Rheumatology, Cardiology, Nephrology, and Endocrinology.

To complement her extensive internal medicine background with international sub-specialty training, she earned a Diploma in Rheumatology from South Wales University, U.K. Her clinical focus includes the advanced management of Rheumatoid Arthritis, Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE), Ankylosing Spondylitis, Osteoporosis, and Gout.

Hospital Panels

Honorary Consultant at Holy Spirit Hospital since 2002; consultant panelist at Nanavati Max, Lilavati, & Bhartiya Arogya Nidhi.

Academic Leadership

D.N.B. postgraduate mentor, CPS medical speaker, and former undergraduate medical teacher at Sion Hospital.

Areas of Expertise

Conditions treated, start to finish
